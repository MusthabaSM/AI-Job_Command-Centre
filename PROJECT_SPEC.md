# AI Alchemist

## Vision

AI Alchemist is an AI-powered Career Operating System that helps students manage every stage of their job search.

Instead of only generating resumes or answering questions, it actively manages applications, analyzes resumes, compares job descriptions, prepares interview plans, and recommends the next best career action.

---

## Target Users

- College students
- Fresh graduates
- Internship seekers
- Early-career professionals

---

## Core Modules

1. Dashboard
2. Resume Center
3. Job Analyzer
4. Resume Optimizer
5. Application Tracker
6. Interview Coach
7. Recruiter Assistant
8. Career Readiness Score

Help users organize and improve their job search using AI-driven workflows rather than isolated chat interactions.
