# AI Alchemist Demo Script

**Estimated Time:** 3 - 5 Minutes

## 1. The Problem (30s)
"Job hunting today is fragmented and overwhelming. You have your resume in Word, your jobs in a messy Excel sheet, and you’re completely guessing at what the ATS wants or what interview questions you’ll face."

## 2. The Solution (30s)
"Welcome to **AI Alchemist** — your personal, AI-native Career Operating System. Instead of a single chatbot, AI Alchemist provides a suite of specialized AI Agents running on a robust Lemma Pod backend."

## 3. Live Demo: Judge Mode (1m)
*(Start at `/login`)*
"To make this easy to evaluate, we built **Judge Mode**. With one click, it bypasses the backend and boots the entire platform with realistic interconnected data."
*(Click 'Enter Judge Mode')*

*(Show Dashboard)*
"Instantly, we see the Career Command Center. The AI has calculated a Readiness Score based on my current pipeline."

## 4. Workflows & Architecture (1m 30s)
*(Navigate to Resume Studio)*
"Our `resume_analyzer` agent processes PDFs into strict JSON, while the `ats_optimizer` actively rewrites bullets to match job descriptions."

*(Navigate to Job Intelligence)*
"Here, the `job_matcher` vector-matches my saved jobs against my parsed resume. It identifies missing skills like GraphQL, and hands that off to the `career_planner`..."

*(Navigate to Mission Control)*
"Which brings us to Mission Control. Here I can chat directly with the `career_planner` or `interview_coach` while watching them mutate the database state in real-time."

## 5. Conclusion (30s)
"AI Alchemist proves that by combining a modern Next.js interface with specialized Lemma Agents, we can fundamentally transform how candidates manage their careers. Thank you."
